## Functional Fix
The journal-posting boundary now validates the complete debit/credit line set before the persistence callback that writes a `POSTED` journal can execute. Entry and line persistence remain inside one Room transaction. Therefore an unbalanced journal or a line-integrity failure aborts before any `POSTED` persistence is reached.

`postManualJournal()` is routed through the new `postJournalEntry()` boundary so its status cannot be persisted as `POSTED` before `AccountingValidator.validate(...)` succeeds.
