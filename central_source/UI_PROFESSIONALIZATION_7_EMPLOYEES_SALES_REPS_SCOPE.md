# UI Professionalization 7 — Employees & Sales Representatives

Version: `0.15.4.40-ui-employees-sales-reps`

## Scope
- Professional HR dashboard with active staff, production staff and competence alerts.
- Search and status filters for employee files.
- Employee profile executive cards for accrued production compensation, paid amount and outstanding amount.
- Clear training and equipment-authorization validity states.
- Professional production-compensation and employee-voucher cards while preserving account 2200 behavior.
- Professional sales-representative dashboard and search/status filters.
- Sales-representative profile executive cards for sales, collections, returns and commission due.
- Clear commission lifecycle: earned, reversed, paid and remaining due.
- Professional commission and voucher cards while preserving account 2300 and existing payment/reversal services.

## Safety boundary
No Room schema, DAO, accounting posting, production labor accrual, employee compensation formula, commission formula, collection rule, return rule, voucher posting or reversal behavior is changed by this UI phase.
