# FUSH ERP Mobile — UI Professionalization 4: Accounting & Treasury

Baseline: `0.15.4.36-ui-purchases-suppliers`
Target: `0.15.4.37-ui-accounting-treasury`

## Scope
Presentation and usability improvements for accounting, treasury, manual journals and financial reports.

## Implemented
- Professional accounting workspace header and navigation hierarchy.
- Journal KPIs, search, clearer entry states and improved table presentation.
- Treasury KPIs and professional cash/bank cards with quick voucher actions.
- Manual journal safety guidance and stronger visual hierarchy.
- Improved report filters, summary cards, empty states and financial status cues.
- Consistent reusable accounting tables and metrics across ledger, trial balance, income statement, balance sheet and cash flow.

## Safety boundary
No journal posting, reversal rules, voucher posting, account balances, DAO queries, report formulas, exchange-rate calculations or database schema are intentionally changed.
