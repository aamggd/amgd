# Phase 14.3 — Monthly & Weekly Sales Budget

- Uses the approved monthly demand plan as the controlled monthly sales target.
- Auto-distributes the monthly target by calendar-day weight into 4 or 5 operating weeks.
- Allows controlled manual weekly redistribution while enforcing that weekly totals equal the approved monthly target.
- Shows weekly and monthly actual net sales quantity after returns.
- Shows weekly and monthly achievement percentages against target.
- Shows actual net sales-line value in base currency.
- Supports Taiz, Aden, Sanaa, and Other province scopes using the existing geography model.
- Records weekly budget creation, redistribution, and edits in the audit log.
- Database migration 15 -> 16 adds only sales_budget_weeks; existing data is preserved.
