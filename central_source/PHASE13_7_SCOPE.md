# Phase 13.7 — Detailed Production Statement

- Detailed production statement button on every production order.
- Order, recipe/version, warehouses, asset/operator and lifecycle dates.
- Planned, actual, accepted, rejected and scrap quantities plus plan/yield percentages.
- Standard/reserved/issued material quantities.
- Historical issued unit cost and material total cost.
- Lot-by-lot issued quantity, unit cost, total, expiry and issue date.
- Material, labor, total production cost and accepted-unit cost.
- Quality checks and CAPA/nonconformance history.
- No database schema change. Existing data is preserved.
