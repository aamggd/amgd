# Phase 14.5.1 — Customer balances and purchase invoice details

- Customer cards show total net credit due after posted returns, amount paid, remaining balance, and overdue part.
- Paid amount is capped per invoice at the invoice net credit due after posted returns so the displayed total, paid, and remaining values reconcile.
- Purchase invoice cards include a **بيان تفصيلي** action.
- Purchase detail shows supplier/system invoice references, date, warehouse, currency/exchange rate, payment type/status, item/unit quantities and conversion, original prices, historical base cost, lot/expiry, returned and remaining quantities, financial totals, net purchases, and posted return history/settlement.
